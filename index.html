Read this before you sit

Take a minute with it. Once you press Begin, you won't need to look again. A calm voice guides the first four minutes: arriving, the breath, a body scan and the question. Then it goes quiet for the meditation and returns briefly for the seal.

    Habits that help

    • Decide the night before: same spot, same time. In the morning, open the app, read for one minute, press Begin, and set the phone face down.
    • You get 10 seconds after Begin to put the phone down and settle. A bell starts the session; three bells end it.
    • Don't lock the phone during the sit. The screen stays on but black so the timing keeps working. If it locks or you switch apps, the session pauses where you left it.
    • Sit on the cushion by default. Kneel if your hips are tight. Lie down only when tired, and on Fire day swap the pumping breath for slow belly breaths.
    • Sitting again later? Repeat the same day. Tap the black screen once to pause or end.
    • If the question in step 4 brings up something heavy, let it be there and return to the breath. You don't need to solve it on the cushion.
